## Context

Epic: `EPIC-08`. This story covers the round's always-on HUD, as specified in
`docs/wiki/design/components.md`:

- **C-14, private turn cues.** A card per live or next machine, showing the tag
  in your hue and an arrow. The arrow points at the machine when you are in its
  room; otherwise it points at the next doorway on a shortest path, with ties
  going to the lower-indexed next room (`mechanics.md` §3.2, Q-G2).
- **C-15, the public progress bar.** Its model's input is exactly
  `{ committed, total }`, the finale segments are marked, and it is never
  animated between commits (PT-P1).
- **C-19, the round clock.** A numeric mm:ss countdown, always visible, showing
  a brief −20 for each penalty. This is the provisional form of T21; the clock
  itself is decided (`tuning.md` §4, G8).
- **C-20, the instability pips.** There are `instability_max` pips, with the
  blackout thresholds marked. Nothing combines the clock with the bar
  (`hud_clock_separate_from_progress`).

**Which required gate would fail if this story's artifact broke:** `unit`. The
screen is `D-1`.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** (PT-P1) — Given `ProgressModel.segments(bar, finaleCount)`, when it is
  called with `{ committed = k, total = 8 }`, then it returns 8 segments, the
  first k filled and the last `procedure_tracks` marked as finale. It raises if
  `bar` has any key other than `committed` and `total`. Nothing it returns
  depends on anything but those two numbers.
  *Control:* passing `{ committed = 3, total = 8, track = 1 }` must raise.
- **AC-2** — Given `TurnCues`, a `FacilityView` and the viewer's position, when
  `TurnCueModel.cards` runs, then:
  - there is one card per cue id;
  - each card's `arrowTarget` is the machine when the viewer is in the
    machine's room, and otherwise the next doorway on a shortest path, with
    ties going to the lower-indexed next room;
  - `live` cards sort before `next` cards, and each group sorts nearest first.

  *Control:* on a fixture where the straight line to the machine crosses a wall
  and the path goes through a door on the far side, a model that points at the
  machine from another room must fail.
- **AC-3** — Given `ClockModel.describe(secondsLeft, lastPenaltyAt, now,
  reducedMotion)`, when it runs, then the text is mm:ss of `secondsLeft`,
  rounded up. The penalty text shows `−` followed by
  `instability_clock_penalty_seconds`, for the duration `tokens.md` specifies
  after a penalty. With `reducedMotion`, nothing is tweened.
- **AC-4** — Given `InstabilityModel.pips(i, max, threshold)`, when it runs,
  then it returns `max` pips, the first `i` filled. Pips at multiples of
  `threshold` below `max` are marked.
- **AC-5** — Given any model in this story, when its output is inspected, then
  no progress output mentions time, and no clock output mentions progress.
  Nothing yields a pace, par or forecast (`hud_clock_separate_from_progress`).

## Contract

The models are pure, in `src/client/models/`, with the signatures in
`components.md`: `ProgressModel`, `TurnCueModel`, `ClockModel` and
`InstabilityModel`. RED may sharpen a signature in place, with a reason.
`TurnCueModel` needs a shortest-path helper over `FacilityView`'s doors. Write
it in `src/client/models/Paths.luau`. **Never require `src/server/facility/Layout`
from the client** (`architecture.md` §1).

The views are `TurnCueView`, `ProgressBarView`, `ClockView` and
`InstabilityView` in `src/client/views/`, placed in the regions of
`layout.md`.

**Oracle partition.**
- AC-1 and AC-4 are **settled** by `mechanics.md` §3.2, §3.3 and
  `components.md`.
- AC-2 is **settled** by `mechanics.md` §3.2 (G2), over **mechanical** paths.
- AC-3 and AC-5 are **settled** by `tuning.md` §4's HUD table.

## Deferred verifications

**D-1. Studio (the operator).** In a 4-client round, check:
- the cues point through doorways;
- the bar fills one segment per commit and jumps by two at the finale;
- the clock drops by 20 on a wrong turn;
- the pips fill, with the thresholds marked;
- SC-L1 and SC-L2 pass.
