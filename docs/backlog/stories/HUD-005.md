## Context

Epic: `EPIC-09`. This story builds the post-round screen, using two components
from `docs/wiki/design/components.md`:

- **C-24, the trace.** It shows an outcome header, the headline sentence, your
  time against the best route, a two-lane timeline and the guesses. It shows no
  player hue anywhere (T14 on the client).
- **C-25, the rematch card.** It is shown during `Post` beside the trace, driven
  by `RoundView.phase`, and acceptance goes on `AcceptRematch`. The machine's
  `PromptRematch` effect, emitted on leaving `Post`, dismisses it.

The screen is shown for `post_round_seconds`.

**Which required gate would fail if this story's artifact broke:** `unit`. The
look is `D-1`.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** — Given `TraceModel.describe(traceView, outcome, now)`, when it runs,
  then it takes no seat or player input, and its output contains no player hue
  token (the structural form of T14).
- **AC-2** — Given a win under par, when described, then the header shows the
  margin. Given a loss, then it shows `reached` as `k of 8`. The words come from
  `voice.md`.
- **AC-3** — Given the timeline, when described, then two lanes (tracks) show
  steps in canonical numbering, and each gap carries its label's pictogram,
  including *waiting for the other pair*.
- **AC-4** (PT-R2) — Given `RematchModel.describe(phase, acceptedIds, viewerId,
  pending)`, when it runs, then the card is visible iff the phase is `Post`. Its
  state is `offer`, `pending` or `accepted` for the viewer. Accepted players are
  shown as hue swatches only, with no names.

## Contract

The models are pure, in `src/client/models/`: `TraceModel` and `RematchModel`,
per `components.md`. The views are `TraceView` and `RematchCardView`. A tap on
Accept calls `ClientTransport.send("AcceptRematch", {})`.

**Oracle partition.** All criteria are **settled** by `components.md`,
`voice.md` and `mechanics.md` §7.

## Deferred verifications

**D-1. Studio (the operator).** In a 4-client round, run it to a win and to a
loss. Check:
- the trace shows for `post_round_seconds`;
- the headline reads as a sentence a 7-year-old could read aloud;
- accepting a rematch on 2 clients shows 2 swatches on all 4;
- everyone returns to the Lobby together.
