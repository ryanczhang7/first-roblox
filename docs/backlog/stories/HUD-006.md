## Context

Epic: `EPIC-10`. This story builds two components from
`docs/wiki/design/components.md`:

- **C-22, the seat-change notice.** After a disconnect it shows "you can turn
  these too" to the inheriting supplier, or "no helper now" to the leaver's
  partner, whose machines must now be guessed (`mechanics.md` §8).
- **C-27, the spectator banner.** It shows "you'll join next round".

Both are computed from the player's own payloads only.

**Which required gate would fail if this story's artifact broke:** `unit`. The
look is `D-1`.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** — Given an old and a new `SeatView`, when `SeatChangeModel.diff` runs,
  then it returns:
  - `inherited` when `keyClasses` grew;
  - `lost_helper` when the supplier is gone, or became the viewer's own
    dependent in a 3-cycle with no lens for the viewer's class;
  - nothing when no relevant field changed.

  These are exactly the cases in C-22.
  *Control:* a diff that fires on a `seatOrder` change alone must fail.
- **AC-2** — Given a `SpectatorView`, when the banner model describes it, then it
  is visible until the next `Lobby`, and the action buttons are disabled
  (`ActionButtonModel` with `isSpectator`).

## Contract

The models are pure: `SeatChangeModel` and `SpectatorModel`, per
`components.md`. The views are `SeatChangeNoticeView` and `SpectatorBannerView`.

**Oracle partition.** All criteria are **settled** by `components.md`.

## Deferred verifications

**D-1. Studio (the operator).** With 4 clients, disconnect one. Check:
- the supplier sees "you can turn these too";
- the partner sees "no helper now";
- after a rejoin within 30 s, both notices clear;
- a rejoin after 30 s shows the spectator banner.

Owner: REVIEW.

## Out of scope
